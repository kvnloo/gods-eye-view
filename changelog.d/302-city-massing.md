- Behind the `?massing=1` URL flag, City builder play mode offers MASSING on
  the selected plot: a ground plate and an assumed block standing on the
  sampled ground, casting its shadow from the scene sun, with a readout of
  height (estimated), storeys, FAR and floor area and a storeys stepper. On
  photorealistic 3D tiles the plot is cut out of the mesh first. The block is
  computed from the plot each time and never saved (Kevin Rajan, kvnloo#302).
