- Behind the `?massing=1` URL flag, City builder play mode shows an assumed
  massing block on the selected plot: a ground plate and a block standing on
  the sampled ground, casting its shadow from the scene sun. On photorealistic
  3D tiles the plot is cut out of the mesh first. The block is computed from
  the plot each time and never saved (Kevin Rajan, kvnloo#302).
