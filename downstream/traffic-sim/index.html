Why traffic jams appear out of nowhere, and how FSD cars dissolve them

Same ring road, same number of cars, no accident and no bottleneck. Human drivers react about 0.7 s late and wobble on the pedal. Each small brake gets copied a bit harder by the car behind, until a stop-and-go wave forms and rolls backwards. FSD cars react almost instantly, watch several cars ahead, and coast into slowdowns instead of braking late. The gap they keep soaks up the wave.
